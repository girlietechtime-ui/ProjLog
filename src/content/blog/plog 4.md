What do you have to do to feel better about yourself?

This is a question I find I ask myself too often. From a practical standpoint, my acheivements lie in between "googoo gaga baby shit" to "manic episode manifested into a physical object". This creates a weird dichotomy of interests, where my projects exist in a bubble, made entirely when I lacked the ability to control myself in one way or another. While my progress on controlling these emotions has improved to the point I can be consistently social with strangers and friends alike, it has brought my so-called project brain to a screeching halt. I seem to have a two-way switch in my mind, where my energy and brainpower gets put towards interacting with people and socializing, or creating projects that feel unique and special. I also have a habit of forgetting that I created projects. As energy is not a unlimited resource, as soon as I run out of energy I crumble. I make mistakes, I stop functioning in crowds, I lose all interest in my projects, and all I want to do is play games or sleep. This can happen at noon, or at midnight, there's no telling how the day will go. This can create a perfect storm where I both run out of energy to give towards upholding my own well being, and fall into the trap of overthinking everyhting that happens around me.
